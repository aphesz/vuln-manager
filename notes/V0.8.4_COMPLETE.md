## Key Metrics & Algorithms

### Risk Scoring Algorithm
- **Critical** = 10 points per finding
- **High** = 7 points per finding
- **Medium** = 4 points per finding
- **Low** = 2 points per finding
- **Informational** = 1 point per finding

### Risk Color Coding
- **Red** (Critical): Risk Score ≥ 50
- **Orange** (High): Risk Score ≥ 30
- **Yellow** (Medium): Risk Score ≥ 15
- **Green** (Low): Risk Score < 15

### MTTR Calculation
```python
MTTR = mean(resolved_at - discovered_at) for all resolved findings
```
- Based on `discovered_at` and `resolved_at` timestamps
- Calculated in days with 2 decimal precision
- Returns 0.0 if no resolved findings

### Trend Direction
- **Improving**: >5% reduction in new findings (30-day comparison)
- **Worsening**: >5% increase in new findings
- **Stable**: -5% to +5% change

### Compliance Coverage
- **OWASP Top 10**: % of findings mapped to `owasp_category` field
- **CWE Top 25**: % of findings with "CWE-" in title/description
- **MITRE ATT&CK**: % of findings with technique IDs in instances
